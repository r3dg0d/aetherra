The AETHER-1 vehicle in ASCENT is fictional. The ascent reconstructions on launch pages are generic SIMULATED profiles, not telemetry. Webcam and livestream entries are links to official operators only.

Generated in-app, with no third-party assets: the Milky Way band (procedural noise in true galactic coordinates), the ASCENT exhaust plume particles, the intro sequence, and every sound effect (Web Audio synthesis).
