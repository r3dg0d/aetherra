# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** (Security tab → Advisories) rather than opening a public issue. Include steps to reproduce and the affected version or commit.

Scope highlights: server-side request forgery through provider or webcam URLs, leaking server-side credentials, push-subscription abuse, and content-security-policy bypasses.

AETHERRA never ingests private or unsecured camera feeds, and does not perform face recognition, licence-plate tracking or cross-source identity correlation. Reports that would require adding such capabilities are out of scope.
